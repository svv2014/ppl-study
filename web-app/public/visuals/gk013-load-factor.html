VaDesign Maneuvering SpeedAt or below Va, full control inputs won't overstress the aircraft — it will stall first

Maneuvering Speed (Va) Rule: Va decreases as aircraft weight decreases. A lighter aircraft will reach critical load factor at a lower airspeed. In turbulence, slow to Va or below and avoid large, abrupt control inputs. The formula for bank load factor: LF = 1 / cos(bank angle). At 60° bank, LF = 1/cos(60°) = 1/0.5 = 2g — and stall speed increases by √2 ≈ 41%.
