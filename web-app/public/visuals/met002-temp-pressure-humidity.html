Cloud-Base Formula
FormulaExample
Base AGL = (T − Td) × 400 ft T = 18 °C, Td = 12 °C → spread 6 °C → cumulus base ≈ 2,400 ft AGL
EXAM HOOK
Lows = Lousy (CCW inflow, rising, cloudy). Highs = Happy (CW outflow, sinking, clear). Temperature–dew-point spread of 2 °C or less means saturation is close — watch for fog. Multiply the spread by 400 ft to estimate a convective cloud base.
