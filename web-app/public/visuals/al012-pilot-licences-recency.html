AL-012 — Pilot Licences & Recency Requirements

Transport Canada · CARs Part 400 · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

Pilot Certificate / Licence Types
SPP
Student Pilot Permit
  • Solo flight only under instructor supervision
  • Minimum age: 14 (aeroplane)
  • Within 25 NM of home aerodrome (solo cross-country with endorsement)
  • Passengers: not permitted
RPP
Recreational Pilot Permit
  • Minimum age: 17
  • Day VFR only
  • Single-engine piston land ≤ 1200 kg
  • One passenger only
  • Within Canada only
PPL
Private Pilot Licence
  • Minimum age: 17
  • Day VFR (night/IFR with additional ratings)
  • Any number of passengers (no hire/reward)
  • Minimum 45 hours total (40 dual + 12 solo)
  • Written exam + flight test required
CPL
Commercial Pilot Licence
  • Minimum age: 18
  • Fly for hire/reward
  • 200 hours minimum total time
  • Includes all PPL privileges
ATPL
Airline Transport Pilot Licence
  • Minimum age: 21
  • Required as PIC on airline operations
  • 1500 hours minimum total time
PPL Recency Requirements (CARs 401.05)

To Carry Passengers as PIC — All Must Be Met

5 h
Flight Time in Previous 6 Months
Must have flown at least 5 hours as pilot-in-command on aeroplanes (or the applicable category/class) within the 6 months before the flight.
5 T&L
5 Takeoffs and Landings in Previous 6 Months
Must have completed 5 takeoffs and landings as PIC in the same category and class of aircraft within the previous 6 months.

Memory Hook — Recency

5 × 6"Five in Six" — 5 hours PIC + 5 T&Ls, all within 6 months. Both conditions must be met to carry passengers.
Cat 3Category 3 medical for PPL — think "3rd level" for private pilots. Valid 5 years if you're under 40, shorter as you age.
