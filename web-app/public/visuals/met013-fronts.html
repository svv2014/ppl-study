EXAM HOOK
Cold front: steep, fast, short & violent — think CBs, gust fronts, sharp wind shift clockwise, pressure rise. Warm front: shallow, slow, long & steady — think CI → CS → AS → NS sequence, low ceilings, rime icing. At any front, expect wind shift and pressure change at passage. Occluded = cold front overtaking warm — hazards of both.
