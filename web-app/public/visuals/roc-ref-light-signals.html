ROC-A — ATC Light Signals

Canadian AIM RAC 4.5.7 · Transport Canada · ROC-A Quick Reference

Used by ATC when radio communication is impossible or has failed. Colour alone does not fully convey meaning — the type of signal (steady, flashing, alternating) is equally important. Acknowledge receipt by rocking wings (day) or flashing landing/navigation lights (night).

Green — cleared / proceed
Red — stop / danger / do not land
White — return / land here (with green)
Alt. Red/Green — extreme caution
Aircraft on the Ground
Signal Type Colour Meaning for Aircraft on Ground
Green STEADY Steady Green #4caf7d Cleared to take off. ATC has approved your departure from the runway in use.
Green FLASHING Flashing Green #4caf7d Cleared to taxi. You may taxi but are NOT cleared for takeoff.
Red STEADY Steady Red #e05050 Stop. Halt immediately. Do not proceed until cleared.
Red FLASHING Flashing Red #e05050 Taxi clear of the runway in use. An aircraft on approach or another runway conflict requires you to clear immediately.
White FLASHING Flashing White #e4ecf5 Return to starting point on the aerodrome. You are directed back to where you began your taxi.
Alt. R/G ALTERNATING Alternating Red & Green #e05050 / #4caf7d Exercise extreme caution. A hazard exists — proceed with maximum vigilance.
Aircraft in Flight
Signal Type Colour Meaning for Aircraft in Flight
Green STEADY Steady Green #4caf7d Cleared to land. The runway is clear and you are authorised for landing.
Green FLASHING Flashing Green #4caf7d Return for landing. Continue to circuit; a steady green will follow when cleared to land.
Red STEADY Steady Red #e05050 Give way to other aircraft and continue circling. Do not land — continue the circuit and await further signals.
Red FLASHING Flashing Red #e05050 Airport unsafe — do not land. A hazard exists on or near the runway; divert or continue circling.
White FLASHING Flashing White #e4ecf5 Land at this aerodrome and proceed to the apron. A flashing green will follow when you may land.
Alt. R/G ALTERNATING Alternating Red & Green #e05050 / #4caf7d Exercise extreme caution. A hazard exists — you may proceed but with maximum vigilance.
Acknowledge receipt: In flight, rock wings (day) or flash landing/navigation lights (night). On the ground, flash landing lights or move ailerons.

Key distinctions: Steady Green on ground = take off; in flight = land. Flashing Green on ground = taxi; in flight = return for landing. Steady Red on ground = stop; in flight = circle and give way.

Source: AIM RAC 4.5.7 — Light Signals from ATC Tower. These signals apply when radio communication cannot be established or has failed.
