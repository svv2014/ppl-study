Special Low-Level Rules
Open-Air Assembly — CARs 602.14
CARs 602.14
An open-air assembly of persons (outdoor concert, fair, sports event) is treated the same as a congested area: 1,000 ft above highest obstacle within 2,000 ft. No fly-overs at low altitude for "fun" or photography.
Noise Abatement Procedures
AIM AIR 1.2
NOTAM or published procedures at specific aerodromes may require noise abatement routing — higher minimum altitudes, turn restrictions, or preferred runways. Compliance is mandatory where published. Does not replace CARs minimums.
Aerobatics Altitude Limit
CARs 602.27
Aerobatic manoeuvres require minimum 2,000 ft AGL over a built-up area, and minimum 1,500 ft AGL elsewhere, unless an air show waiver is in effect.
