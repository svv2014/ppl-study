AL-011 — Canadian Aviation Regulations (CARs) Structure

Transport Canada · CARs SOR/96-433 · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

Regulatory Hierarchy
Aeronautics Act
Federal statute — the parent law enabling all aviation regulation in Canada
CARs
Canadian Aviation Regulations (SOR/96-433) — day-to-day rules
Divided into numbered Parts → Subparts → Divisions
Standards
Technical standards attached to each Part — more detail, may be amended more easily
AIM / TP Docs
Aeronautical Information Manual and TP publications — guidance, not law
Key CARs Parts for PPL Exam
100
General Provisions
Definitions, abbreviations, and interpretation rules used throughout the CARs
200
Aircraft Registration & Marking
Registration certificates, nationality marks (C-), aircraft markings
300
Aerodromes & Airports
Aerodrome standards, certification, lighting, markings, reporting
400
Personnel Licensing & Training
Pilot licences, medical standards, rating requirements, recency
500
Airworthiness
Type certificates, airworthiness directives, maintenance release
600
General Operating & Flight Rules
VFR/IFR rules, altitudes, right-of-way, weather minima — most exam questions
700
Air Carrier Operations
Commercial air services, crew requirements, flight/duty time
800
Dangerous Goods
Transport of hazardous materials by air — classification, packaging
