Instruments at a Glance

Failure Effects
BlockageEffect on Instruments
Pitot blocked (ice/insect)ASI reads zero or freezes; ALT and VSI unaffected
Static blockedALT and VSI freeze; ASI reads incorrect (over/under reads with altitude change)
Both blockedAll three instruments unreliable
ASI Speed Arcs (Colour Code)
White arcVso to Vfe — flap operating range
Green arcVs1 to Vno — normal operating range
Yellow arcVno to Vne — caution (smooth air only)
Red lineVne — never exceed speed
Pitot Heat: Turn on pitot heat before entering visible moisture (clouds, rain, freezing temps) to prevent ice from blocking the pitot tube. A blocked pitot in IMC is extremely dangerous. The alternate static source (if installed) bypasses a blocked static port — opens to cockpit interior pressure, which causes slight ASI over-read and ALT over-read.
