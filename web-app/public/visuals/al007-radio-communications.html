FrequencyPurposeNotes

Standard Phraseology — Key Words
WordMeaningCommon Exam Trap
Roger I have received all of your last transmission Does NOT mean you will comply — use Wilco for that
Wilco Understood and will comply Implies Roger — don't say both
Affirmative Yes Don't use "yeah", "yep", or informal equivalents
Negative No Don't use "nope" or informal equivalents

Common Exam Traps

Roger ≠ Comply"Roger" confirms you received the message. "Wilco" means you will comply. A landing clearance requires a full readback — not just "Roger".
Say AgainUse "Say Again" — not "Repeat". "Repeat" has a specific military meaning (fire again) and is non-standard in civilian aviation.
121.5 MHzThe emergency frequency is 121.5 MHz — all aircraft should monitor it when not on another working frequency. It is the frequency for Mayday and Pan-Pan calls.
UncontrolledAt uncontrolled aerodromes you are broadcasting position and intentions on 122.8 MHz MF, not requesting clearance. No ATC present.
NinerThe digit 9 is pronounced "niner" in aviation to avoid confusion with the German word for "no" (nein).
