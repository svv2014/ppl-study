Airspace Ceiling / Floor Notation

Reading the Fraction

Airspace extents are shown as CEILING/FLOOR in hundreds of feet ASL.

The top number is the ceiling. The lower number is the floor.
SFC means the airspace begins at the surface.

40 / SFC

Surface up to 4,000 ft ASL

Common Examples

30/SFC — Surface to 3,000 ft ASL
40/15 — 1,500 ft to 4,000 ft ASL
100/40 — 4,000 ft to 10,000 ft ASL
UNL/SFC — Surface to unlimited

Navigational Aids (NAVAIDs)
VOR
VHF Omnidirectional Range
  • Compass-rose circle with dot
  • Printed: frequency, 3-letter ID, Morse
  • Provides magnetic radials 000°–360°
  • Used for en-route and approach nav
NDB
Non-Directional Beacon
  • Purple/magenta dot + dashed circle
  • Printed: frequency, 2–3 letter ID, Morse
  • Transmits in all directions (LF/MF band)
  • Used with ADF — less precise than VOR
Maximum Elevation Figure (MEF)

What is the MEF?

The MEF is the highest known elevation in each 30-minute latitude/longitude grid square on the VNC. It includes terrain, towers, antennas, and a buffer above the highest obstacle.

Shown as a large bold number in the centre of each grid square.

Fly above the MEF for guaranteed terrain clearance over the entire grid square.

Reading the MEF

47
Bold number in grid square

Read as 4,700 feet ASL
The leading digit(s) are thousands, trailing digit is hundreds.
E.g. "47" = 4,700 ft · "12" = 1,200 ft · "9" = 900 ft

Memory Hook — Commonly Tested Chart Symbols

AIRPORT COLOURS Blue = tower, Yellow = no tower, Magenta = water — think: blue for busy (controlled), yellow for mellow (uncontrolled).
AIRSPACE LINES Solid blue = Class C · Dashed blue = Class D · Dashed magenta = Class E surface — solid means strict (need clearance), dashed means less strict.
MEF FORMULA 1 cm = 5 km on the VNC (1:500,000) — 6 cm apart on chart = 30 km on the ground. The number in each grid square = MSL ceiling in hundreds of feet.
CEILING / FLOOR On a VNC, airspace extent is shown as CEILING over FLOOR. "30/SFC" = surface up to 3,000 ft ASL. Top number is always ceiling.
ISOGONIC LINE Dashed purple line = magnetic variation line. In eastern Canada it runs roughly north–south, ~10–15° West. Use it to convert True → Magnetic by adding westerly variation.
VOR vs NDB VOR = blue compass rose + dot (precise, VHF). NDB = purple dot + dashed ring (lower frequency, less precise). Exam may ask you to identify which symbol belongs to which NAVAID.
