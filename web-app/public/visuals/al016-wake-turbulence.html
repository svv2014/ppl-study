Avoidance Rules
▶ Landing behind heavy: Stay above the heavy's approach path and land beyond its touchdown point. Wait at least 2 minutes if same runway.
▶ Departing behind heavy: Rotate before the heavy's rotation point and turn to avoid its flight path. Vortices begin at rotation.
▶ Crossing traffic: Fly above the flight path of the preceding aircraft, not below it, as vortices sink.
▶ Crosswind caution: A light crosswind can hold the upwind vortex near the centreline. Allow extra separation in calm or light wind conditions.

Memory Hook

SINKVortices always sink and drift downwind — fly above and upwind of a preceding heavy aircraft.
2 MINLight behind heavy, same runway = 2 minutes minimum wait after the heavy departs or lands.
ROTATIONVortices start at rotation on departure, at touchdown on landing. The zone in between is clear.
