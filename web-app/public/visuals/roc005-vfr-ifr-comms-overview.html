ROC-005 — VFR / IFR Communications Overview

AIM COM 4.0 · CARs Part VI · ISED RIC-21 · ROC-A Written Exam

VFR vs IFR at a Glance

VFR Communications

  • Radio required in Class B, C, D airspace and at MF aerodromes
  • Class E and G: no radio required (recommended)
  • Self-announce at non-towered (MF) aerodromes on 122.8 MHz
  • No ATC clearance required in Class D — radio contact only
  • VFR flight plans reported to FSS; not mandatory but strongly recommended
  • ATIS must be obtained before contacting tower at controlled airports
  • Transponder required in Class B and C (Mode C); recommended elsewhere

IFR Communications

  • Radio mandatory at all times while operating IFR
  • ATC clearance required before entering controlled airspace IFR
  • Position reports at all compulsory reporting points
  • Must acknowledge all ATC instructions and read back clearances
  • Transponder Mode C required in all controlled airspace
  • Lost communications procedures defined: squawk 7600, attempt 121.5
  • Clearance delivery before taxi at large airports

EXAM HOOK
VFR radio rule: Radio required in B, C, D, and at MF aerodromes. Not required in E or G (but strongly recommended). IFR: always on radio, always on ATC, always read back clearances. ATIS first — then call ATC at any towered airport.
