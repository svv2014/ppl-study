Fuel Grades
GradeColourUse
AVGAS 100LL Blue Most common aviation gasoline. Low-lead. Used in piston engines requiring 100 octane.
AVGAS 80 Red Lower octane — only for engines specifically approved for 80 octane.
Mogas (auto) Clear/Yellow Only if STC (Supplemental Type Certificate) approves it for that aircraft. Not standard.
Jet-A / Jet B Clear/Straw Turbine (jet/turboprop) fuel ONLY. Fatal if used in piston engine.

Critical Safety Rule: Always check for water contamination by draining fuel from ALL sump points before flight. Water is denser than AVGAS and sinks to the bottom — it will stop the engine. Never fly immediately after refuelling without re-draining (temperature changes can cause condensation). The fuel selector must be on BOTH (or the fuller tank) for takeoff and landing — as specified in the POH.
