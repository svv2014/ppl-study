MET-014 — Weather Decision-Making

Transport Canada · TP 12880E · CARs 602.71 · Pre-flight & in-flight decision framework

Decision Flow

1 · Gather

METAR, TAF, GFA (Clouds & Weather + Icing/Turb), SIGMETs, AIRMETs, PIREPs. Cover whole route plus alternates.

2 · Check CARs 602.71 — Pre-flight Information

Legal requirement: the PIC must be familiar with all available weather information relevant to the flight before take-off.

3 · Test against regulatory minima

VFR minima by airspace (CAR 602.114/602.115), destination/alternate (for flight plan), fuel reserve. If fail → no-go.

4 · Test against personal minima

Your own limits on ceiling, vis, wind, crosswind, turbulence, night, terrain. These are tighter than legal minima — if any fails → no-go.

5 · Run IMSAFE self-check

If you fail any letter, you are unfit to fly regardless of the weather.

6 · Identify outs

Alternates, reversals, VFR-on-top option, fuel reserve to reach next suitable aerodrome. If no out → no-go.

7 · In flight: re-evaluate every 30 min

Update METAR/TAF via FSS/NAV CANADA, watch for trend vs forecast. A divergence is a trigger to divert — don't press on.

EXAM HOOK
The PIC is legally required by CARs 602.71 to have all available weather information before departure. Apply two tests: regulatory minima (hard floor) AND personal minima (your floor). Add IMSAFE for the pilot. Always have an out. The most dangerous attitude is press-on-itis — when "just a few more miles" kills experienced pilots.
