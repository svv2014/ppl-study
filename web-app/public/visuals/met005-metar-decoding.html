EXAM HOOK
A ceiling is the lowest BKN or OVC layer (FEW/SCT do not count). Wind direction in METARs is true (ATIS/tower gives magnetic). Heights in sky groups are AGL at the reporting aerodrome, in hundreds of feet. Temperatures use M for minus, never a minus sign.
