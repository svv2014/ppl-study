ROC-002 — Standard Phraseology & Readback

ISED RIC-21 · AIM COM 4.0 · ICAO Annex 10 Vol. II · ROC-A Written Exam

Standard Initial Call — 4-Part Format
Part 1
Who you are calling
"Ottawa Tower"
Part 2
Who you are
"Cessna Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot"
Part 3
Where you are
"ten miles south"
Part 4
What you want
"request landing"
Example Exchange
ATC"Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot, Ottawa Tower, runway two five, cleared to land, wind two four zero at one zero."
Pilot"Runway two five, cleared to land, Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot."

Standard Acknowledgement Words
Word / PhraseMeaning
RogerI have received your last transmission (does not confirm compliance)
WilcoUnderstood; will comply (replaces "Roger" when compliance is intended)
AffirmativeYes / that is correct
NegativeNo / that is not correct
StandbyWait — I cannot respond immediately
Say againRepeat your last transmission (never "repeat")
CorrectionAn error was made; the correct version follows
OverMy transmission is complete; I expect a response
OutConversation complete; no reply expected

Memory Hook

MUSTAlways read back: runway, clearance, heading, altitude, altimeter, squawk, frequency
!"Roger" means received — NOT compliance. Use "Wilco" to confirm you will comply.
!Never say "repeat" on aviation radio — that is an artillery term. Say say again.
!Never say "over and out" — "over" expects a reply; "out" ends the call. Pick one.
