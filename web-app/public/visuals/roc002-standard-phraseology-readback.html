ROC-002 — Standard Phraseology & Readback

ISED RIC-21 · AIM COM 4.0 · ICAO Annex 10 Vol. II · ROC-A Written Exam

Standard Initial Call Format

Structure — say it in this order every time

Part 1
Who you're calling
"Ottawa Tower"
Part 2
Who you are
"Cessna Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot"
Part 3
Where you are
"ten miles south"
Part 4
What you want
"request landing"
Example Radio Exchange
ATC "Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot, Ottawa Tower, runway two five, cleared to land, wind two four zero at one zero."
Pilot "Runway two five, cleared to land, Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot."
After initial contact ATC will use abbreviated callsigns. A pilot may abbreviate only after ATC does so first. Once established, use the same abbreviation ATC uses. (AIM COM 4.1.2)
Mandatory Readback Items
InstructionReadbackExample readback
Runway in use / take-off / landing clearance Mandatory "Runway three six, cleared for takeoff, C-GCDF"
Cleared to cross active runway Mandatory "Cross runway two five, C-GCDF"
Holding short of active runway Mandatory "Hold short runway two five, C-GCDF"
Assigned heading Mandatory "Turn left heading two seven zero, C-GCDF"
Assigned altitude / FL Mandatory "Climb and maintain five thousand five hundred, C-GCDF"
Altimeter setting Mandatory "QNH two niner niner two, C-GCDF"
Transponder code (squawk) Mandatory "Squawk four five three two, C-GCDF"
Frequency change Mandatory "One two two decimal eight, C-GCDF, good day"
Taxi route instructions Recommended Read back turns and hold-short points
Standard Acknowledgement Words
Word / PhraseMeaning
RogerI have received your last transmission (not an acknowledgement of compliance)
WilcoI understand and will comply (replaces both "Roger" and "Affirmative" + intent)
AffirmativeYes / that is correct
NegativeNo / that is not correct
StandbyI must pause — wait for further transmission
Say againRepeat your last transmission (never "repeat" — that is an artillery term)
CorrectionAn error was made; correct version follows
OverMy transmission is complete and I expect a response
OutConversation is complete — no response expected
EXAM HOOK
Readback rule: Always read back runway, clearance, heading, altitude, altimeter, squawk, and frequency. "Roger" does NOT confirm compliance — use "Wilco" if you intend to comply. Never say "repeat" on aviation radio — say say again.
