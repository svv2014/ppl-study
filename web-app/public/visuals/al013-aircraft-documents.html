AL-013 — Aircraft Documents Required on Board

Transport Canada · CARs 605.03 · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

The ARROW Acronym — Required Documents
A   R   R   O   W
A
Airworthiness Certificate
A valid Certificate of Airworthiness issued by Transport Canada. Must be carried on board. No expiry date — valid as long as the aircraft is maintained in an airworthy condition and passes required inspections.
CARs 605.03(1)(b)
R
Registration Certificate
Proof the aircraft is registered in Canada. Shows owner information, registration marks (C-XXXX). Must be on board — not required to be displayed but must be produced on request. No expiry — valid until ownership changes or aircraft is deregistered.
CARs 202.27 / 605.03(1)(a)
R
Radio Licence (Station Licence)
An aeronautical radio station licence issued under the Radiocommunication Act. Required on board whenever the aircraft is equipped with radio communication equipment — applies to all flights, domestic and international.
CARs 605.03(1)(d) / Radiocommunication Act
O
Operating Limitations / POH
The aircraft Flight Manual (AFM) or Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH), or equivalent approved document containing operating limitations. Must be accessible to the flight crew. Includes performance data, checklists, and limitations. Must be kept current with all amendments.
CARs 605.84
W
Weight & Balance
Current weight and balance documentation — either in the flight manual or a separate approved document. Includes empty weight, CG datum, and equipment list. Must reflect the actual aircraft configuration; updated whenever equipment is added or removed.
CARs 605.92
Journey Log Requirements (CARs 605.94)

What Must Be Recorded in the Journey Log

Date of each flight
Aircraft registration and type
Name of PIC (and other crew if required)
Departure and destination aerodromes
Time of departure and arrival (block times)
Total flight time for each leg
Any defects identified during the flight
Maintenance release signature after any maintenance
Airworthiness — Key Rules
Maintenance Release
Required after maintenance
Any maintenance work must be followed by a signed maintenance release in the journey log before the aircraft can fly again (CARs 571.10)
Annual Inspection
Every 12 months
Canadian-registered aircraft must have a maintenance review (annual inspection) at least once every 12 months (CARs 625)
Airworthiness Directives
Mandatory compliance
ADs issued by TC must be complied with. Aircraft cannot be flown with an outstanding AD that prohibits flight
Defects
Must be recorded
Any defect found during flight must be entered in the journey log. PIC decides if the defect affects airworthiness before next flight
On Board vs. Records Only
DocumentMust Be On Board?Notes
Certificate of Airworthiness Yes Always on board during flight
Registration Certificate Yes Must be produced on demand by an inspector
Radio Station Licence Yes * * Required on board when aircraft is equipped with radio communication equipment (domestic and international)
POH / Flight Manual Yes Must be accessible to crew in flight
Weight & Balance data Yes Must reflect current aircraft config
Journey Log Yes Must accompany the aircraft on every flight

Memory Hook

Journey LogThink of the journey log as the aircraft's diary — every trip, every defect, every maintenance event is recorded. It travels with the aircraft.
