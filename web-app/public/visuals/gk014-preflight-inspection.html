GK-014 — Preflight Inspection Walkaround

Transport Canada · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

1 Cockpit / Pre-Walk
Documents check (ARROW — see below)
Fuel quantity — check gauges vs. visual
Oil quantity — dipstick check (min qty)
Flight controls — free and correct
Instruments, switches, circuit breakers
Brakes — set parking brake
2 Left Wing
Fuel cap — secure; correct colour fuel
Fuel sump drain — check for water
Leading edge — no dents, damage
Aileron — hinges, freedom of movement
Flap — condition, attachment
Navigation light — lens intact
Left main gear — tire condition, wear
3 Nose / Engine
Propeller — nicks, cracks, security
Spinner — secure
Oil level — correct grade, no leaks
Cowling — latched, no damage
Nose gear / nosewheel — tire, strut
Air inlets — clear of obstructions
Pitot tube — clear of insects/covers
4 Right Wing
Mirror of left wing checks
Fuel sump drain — check for water
Fuel cap — secure
Right main gear — tire, brake
5 Empennage (Tail)
Horizontal stabilizer — no damage
Elevators — free movement, no play
Trim tab — condition and attachment
Vertical fin — condition
Rudder — movement, hinges
Antennas — secure, no damage
Static wicks — condition
ARROW — Required Aircraft Documents
ARROW — Must be on board for every flight (CAR 605.03)
A
Airworthiness Certificate — issued by Transport Canada; valid as long as aircraft maintained per CARs
R
Registration Certificate — aircraft registration (C-FXXX); must match aircraft markings
R
Radio Station Licence — required for any radio equipment on board
O
Operating Handbook (POH/AFM) — Pilot's Operating Handbook or Aircraft Flight Manual approved for that specific aircraft
W
Weight & Balance — current W&B data for the aircraft (may be part of the POH)
Exam Tip: The preflight walkaround is not just procedural — it is legally required under CAR 602.72 (pilot-in-command must ensure airworthiness before flight). If the aircraft has a defect listed in the journey log, it must be assessed against the MEL (Minimum Equipment List) before flight. Always drain fuel sumps after fueling, and after temperature changes that could cause condensation. The ARROW acronym is a frequent exam topic — know all five documents.
