ROC-007 — Light Gun Signals

CARs 602.101 · AIM RAC 4.5.5 · ICAO Annex 2 · ROC-A Written Exam

Light signals are used when radio communication is unavailable or impractical. The tower light gun is authoritative — obey even if you have radio contact. Colour is the primary cue; steadiness indicates immediacy vs. conditionality. (CARs 602.101)

Colour is the primary cue: Green = go/proceed · Red = stop/danger · White = administrative (return to starting point). Steady = immediate action. Flashing = conditional or upcoming action. Alternating red/green = extreme caution regardless of phase.

Memory Hook — 6 Signals × 2 Phases

Steady GIn flight: cleared to land · On ground: cleared take-off
Flash GIn flight: return for landing · On ground: cleared to taxi
Steady RIn flight: give way, keep circling · On ground: stop
Flash RIn flight: aerodrome unsafe, don't land · On ground: clear runway
Flash WIn flight: return to starting point · On ground: return to starting point
Alt R/GExtreme caution — both phases
