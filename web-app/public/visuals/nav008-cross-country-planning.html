NAV-008 — Cross-Country Planning

Transport Canada · CARs 602.34, 602.73 · TP 12880E Ch.9 · PPL Written Exam

Eight-Step Planning Workflow
1

Study Route and Airspace

Examine VNC/WAC for airspace, terrain, restricted areas (Class F), and suitable alternate aerodromes. Mark controlled zone boundaries.

2

Select Cruise Altitude

Apply the VFR cruising altitude rule (CARs 602.34). Must be at least 1,000 ft above terrain (500 ft built-up areas).

→ See altitude table below
3

Measure True Track & Distance

Use a plotter on the VNC. Read true bearing at meridian crossing. Measure distance with plotter scale or dividers.

4

Obtain Weather Briefing

Get METAR, TAF, GFA (Graphical Forecast), winds aloft (FD), and NOTAMs for departure, en route, and destination.

5

Solve Wind Triangle (E6B)

Using winds aloft at planned altitude: calculate True Heading, Wind Correction Angle, and Ground Speed for each leg.

6

Calculate Fuel

En route fuel (using GS) + 30-min day VFR reserve. Verify usable fuel ≥ total required. Plan fuel stop if needed.

→ CARs 602.88
7

File VFR Flight Plan (if required)

Required for flights beyond 25 NM from departure aerodrome or when overflying sparsely populated areas. File with Nav Canada.

→ CARs 602.73
8

Pre-Departure Checks

Complete PLOG, aircraft documents (AROW), fuel and oil check, weather go/no-go decision, notify a responsible person.

VFR Cruising Altitudes — CARs 602.34
Magnetic TrackDirection (approx.)Required VFR AltitudeExamples
000° to 179° Northbound / Eastbound Odd thousands + 500 ft 3,500 · 5,500 · 7,500 · 9,500 ft
180° to 359° Southbound / Westbound Even thousands + 500 ft 4,500 · 6,500 · 8,500 · 10,500 ft

Applies above 3,000 ft AGL. Below 3,000 ft AGL, the rule does not apply but good airmanship recommends following it. Rule uses magnetic track, not true track.

Weather Products — What to Get
METAR
Aviation Routine Weather Report

Current surface conditions at a station. Check departure and destination. Valid at time of observation.

TAF
Terminal Aerodrome Forecast

24–30 hr forecast for airport area. Check destination for arrival window. Note TEMPO and BECMG groups.

GFA
Graphical Forecast for Aviation

En route weather (clouds, precipitation, icing, turbulence). Covers large areas every 6 hours.

FD / FB
Winds and Temps Aloft Forecast

Winds at your planned altitude. Use to solve wind triangle. Given in True degrees; convert to Magnetic.

NOTAM
Notice to Air Missions

Temporary airspace restrictions, closed runways, unlit cranes. Check departure, en route, destination.

PIREP
Pilot Weather Report

Real-time reports from other pilots. Valuable for icing, turbulence, or cloud tops at your planned altitude.

Exam Memory Hooks

ALTITUDENorth/East = Odd+500, South/West = Even+500. Memory hook: "NEO" — North-East-Odd. Or picture a compass: going north is climbing the "odd" side.
FLIGHT PLANVFR flight plan is not legally mandatory for local flights but IS required for cross-country (>25 NM from departure). SAR cannot be alerted if you're overdue without one.
WEATHERGet weather within 2 hours of departure. METARs older than 1 hour at destination should be supplemented with a TAF. Use the most pessimistic forecast for go/no-go decisions.
