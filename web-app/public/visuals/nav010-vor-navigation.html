NAV-010 — VOR Navigation

Transport Canada · TP 12880E Ch.9 · AIM RAC 1.5 · PPL Written Exam

VOR Station · Radials · CDI Instrument Faces
VOR Terminology
TermStands ForDefinition
Radial—A magnetic bearing measured FROM the VOR station outward (e.g. R-045 is northeast of station)
OBSOmnibearing SelectorKnob that selects the course (radial) you want to track or intercept
CDICourse Deviation IndicatorNeedle showing whether aircraft is left or right of selected course; each dot ≈ 2° off course
TO Flag—Station is ahead of you on the selected course; flying the OBS heading takes you toward the VOR
FROM Flag—Station is behind you on the selected course; flying the OBS heading takes you away from the VOR
Cone of Silence—Small area directly overhead the VOR where signal is unreliable; CDI may fluctuate and flag may show OFF
Full-scale deflection—CDI pegged at edge = 10° or more off the selected course
Procedures

Intercepting a Radial

  • 1. Tune and identify the VOR (listen for Morse ident)
  • 2. Twist OBS to the desired radial
  • 3. Determine if TO or FROM and which side of course you are on
  • 4. Turn to an intercept heading (30–45° cut toward the needle)
  • 5. As CDI centers, turn to track the course
  • 6. Apply wind correction angle to maintain centered CDI

Tracking a Radial

  • Establish a wind correction angle (WCA) — try 10° initially
  • If CDI drifts, bracket and halve: double the correction, then halve when back on course
  • Keep corrections small — max 20° WCA in light winds
  • OBS setting does NOT change — only aircraft heading changes
  • Recheck station ident every 10–15 min en route
  • Station passage: flag flips FROM→TO, CDI swings
Memory Hook
Memory Hook
  • Radials are FROM: A radial always goes FROM the station. R-090 is the line going east from the VOR. You are on R-090 when you are east of the station.
  • OBS ≠ heading: The OBS sets the course line, not your heading. You fly a different heading to correct for wind while tracking the OBS course.
  • Cone of silence: When directly overhead, the CDI goes crazy and the flag may go to OFF — this is normal. Note the time and continue.
  • CDI "fly toward the needle": Needle deflected right = turn right to get back on course.
