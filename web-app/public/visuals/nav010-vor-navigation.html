NAV-010 — VOR Navigation

Transport Canada · TP 12880E Ch.9 · AIM COM 3.0 · PPL Written Exam

VOR Instrument — Components
①
OBS (Omni Bearing Selector) — Rotary knob that sets the course you want to track. Dial in the desired radial or inbound course. Clicking around changes the number displayed at top.
②
Deviation Dots — Each dot = 2° of course deviation. Full-scale deflection (5 dots) = 10° off course. The needle shows you which side of the selected course you are on.
③
CDI Needle — Deflected right means the selected course is to your right. Fly toward the needle to return to course.
④
TO / FROM Flag — TO = flying toward the station on the selected course. FROM = flying away from station. Flag reverses when you pass overhead.
Radials, Courses, and TO/FROM
ScenarioFlagOBS SetWhat it means
Flying inbound on the 090 radial TO 270° (reciprocal of 090) You are east of the VOR, flying westbound toward it
Tracking outbound on the 270 radial FROM 270° You are west of the VOR, flying westbound away from it
Passing directly overhead VOR FLAG OFF Any Brief flag removal indicates station passage; needle may swing

Intercepting and Tracking a Radial — Inbound

  1. Identify VOR by Morse code audio
  2. Set desired inbound course on OBS
  3. Check flag = TO
  4. Turn to intercept course; fly toward needle
  5. When needle centers, fly the set course
  6. Apply wind correction to stay on track

Determining Position (Cross-Fix)

  1. Tune and identify VOR #1; centre needle (FROM)
  2. Read OBS — this is the radial you are on
  3. Draw that radial on chart from VOR #1
  4. Tune and identify VOR #2; repeat
  5. Draw second radial — intersection = your position
  6. One VOR + DME distance also gives a fix

Exam Memory Hooks

RADIALSRadials go FROM the station — the 090 radial extends eastbound from the VOR. To fly inbound on the 090 radial, set 270° on OBS (reciprocal). Flag should read TO.
CDIFly toward the needle — if needle is right, turn right. Valid only when TO/FROM flag matches your intent. A FROM flag with a right needle means the course is to your right flying away from station.
IDENTAlways identify the VOR by Morse code before using it for navigation. Many VORs are co-located with ILS or TACAN — wrong freq = wrong station. Listen for the 3-letter ident.
