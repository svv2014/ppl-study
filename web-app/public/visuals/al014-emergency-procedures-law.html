AL-014 — Emergency Procedures: Distress and Urgency

Transport Canada · CARs 602.135 · AIM COM 5.0 · TP 12880E Ch.11 · PPL Written Exam

Emergency Signal Types
MAYDAY
Distress
Life-threatening emergency

Declare when the aircraft or persons on board are in grave and imminent danger requiring immediate assistance.

Spoken three times: MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY

Examples: engine failure over water, fire on board, pilot incapacitation, structural failure.

PAN-PAN
Urgency
Serious but not immediately life-threatening

Declare when there is a serious but not yet life-threatening situation — the safety of the aircraft or persons is at risk but immediate assistance is not required at this instant.

Spoken three times: PAN-PAN PAN-PAN PAN-PAN

Examples: lost communications, medical issue on board (non-critical), fuel concern with airport in range.

Distress vs Urgency — Key Differences
Factor Distress (MAYDAY) Urgency (PAN-PAN)
Severity Grave and imminent danger Serious but not immediate threat
ATC Priority Highest — all other traffic holds High — expedited handling
Squawk code 7700 7700 (if declared emergency) or assigned
Frequency 121.5 MHz (emergency) or any assigned frequency
Legal source CARs 602.135 — pilot's responsibility to declare
Emergency Transponder Codes
7700
General Emergency
Any emergency situation — distress or urgency. Set when declaring MAYDAY or PAN-PAN.
7600
Radio Failure (NORDO)
Lost communications with ATC. Proceed to destination per regulations; ATC will look for light signals.
7500
Hijacking / Unlawful Interference
Set only if subject to unlawful seizure. ATC will alert authorities immediately.

Standard MAYDAY Call — What to Say

1MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY — spoken three times to identify as distress
2Callsign — the aircraft's radio callsign (e.g. "Golf-Foxtrot Alpha Bravo Charlie")
3Nature of emergency — e.g. "engine failure", "fire on board", "pilot incapacitated"
4Position — bearing and distance from a known fix, or latitude/longitude if known
5Altitude — current altitude
6Heading — current magnetic heading
7Pilot's intentions — e.g. "proceeding to CYWG", "forced landing on highway 1"
8Squawk 7700 — set transponder emergency code and ident if able
