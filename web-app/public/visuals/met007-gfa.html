MET-007 — GFA (Graphical Forecast for Aviation)

Transport Canada · NAV CANADA · TP 12880E Chapter 8 · PPL Written Exam

What is a GFA?

The Graphical Forecast for Aviation (GFA) replaced the old written FA (Area Forecast) in Canada. Issued by NAV CANADA's Meteorological Service, GFAs are colour-coded graphical charts covering weather conditions across Canadian regions. Updated 4 times per day, they provide forecasts at multiple valid times up to 24 hours ahead.

GFA Chart Types — Two Charts Per Region
Chart 1: Clouds & Weather
Shows
Cloud coverage and types (BKN, OVC, SCT), cloud base and tops, precipitation areas, visibility restrictions, freezing precipitation, thunderstorm areas
Key use
Go/no-go decision; VFR ceiling and visibility assessment; identifying IFR conditions
Chart 2: Icing, Freezing Level & Turbulence
Shows
Freezing level altitude (0°C isotherm), icing areas with intensity, turbulence areas with intensity, mountain wave activity
Key use
Icing risk assessment; turbulence avoidance altitude selection; identifying safe altitude bands

GFA Pre-Flight Checklist — What to Look For
  • 1
    Freezing Level — Where is the 0°C isotherm? Are you flying above or below? Icing possible from surface to that level in cloud.
  • 2
    Icing Areas — Look for shaded icing regions on Chart 2. Note intensity (LGT, MOD, SEV) and altitude band.
  • 3
    Turbulence — Check for moderate or severe turbulence areas, especially near mountains or frontal zones. Note altitude affected.
  • 4
    Cloud Bases and Tops — Identify ceiling heights along route. BKN or OVC below 1,000 ft = IFR. Check tops to determine if VFR on top is feasible.
  • 5
    Visibility — Look for areas of precipitation or fog reducing visibility. Match to METAR/TAF for destination.
  • 6
    Thunderstorm Areas — TS marked on Clouds & Weather chart. Plan to avoid by at least 20 NM.
  • 7
    Valid Time Match — Select the GFA valid time closest to your ETA at each waypoint. Don't use 0-hour chart for a flight 5 hours away.

Memory Hook — GFA Essentials

GFA REPLACED FAThe old written Area Forecast (FA) is gone in Canada. GFA is the standard — graphical, updated 4x/day at 0000/0600/1200/1800 UTC.
TWO CHARTSAlways look at both: Clouds & Wx for ceiling/visibility, Icing/Turbulence for altitude hazards. Never use just one.
FREEZING LEVELThe 0°C level is on Chart 2. If you fly in cloud below that altitude, expect icing. It shifts with temperature — recheck for long flights.
VALID TIMEGFA covers 0, 6, 12, 18, 24 hrs. Use the chart whose valid time matches your ETA, not your departure time.
