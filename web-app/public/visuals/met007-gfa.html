GFA Rules of Thumb
RuleDetail
Altitudes AGL unless tagged MSLTops usually MSL, bases usually AGL — read labels carefully.
Use two charts togetherCLDWX tells you what you'll fly through; ICG TURBC tells you what will fly back at you.
Cross-check with SIGMETs / AIRMETs / PIREPsGFA is baseline; SIGMETs flag hazards that developed since issue.
IFO is text-only+24 hr outlook is general (no chart).
EXAM HOOK
Every GFA issue contains two charts at three valid times (now, +6, +12), plus a text IFO outlook at +24. Issued four times daily. For a complete pre-flight picture you also need current METAR/TAF, SIGMET/AIRMET, PIREP — GFA is the map, the others are the updates.
