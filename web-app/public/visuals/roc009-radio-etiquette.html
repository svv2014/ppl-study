ROC-009 — Radio Etiquette

ISED RIC-21 · AIM COM 4.0 · ICAO Annex 10 Vol. II · ROC-A Written Exam

Core Rules
Rule 1

Listen before transmitting

Monitor the frequency for at least 5 seconds before pressing PTT. Transmitting over someone causes both transmissions to be lost ("stepped on").

Rule 2

Keep transmissions brief

Plan what you will say before keying PTT. Avoid fillers, mid-transmission pauses, and unnecessary repetition. State who, where, what — then release.

Rule 3

Use standard phraseology

ICAO standard words carry precise meaning. Plain language is permitted when standard phrases don't fit, but standard phrases reduce ambiguity and speed exchanges.

Rule 4

Speak at a moderate pace

Conversational speed — not fast (hard to write down), not slow (wastes airtime). Slow slightly for alphanumeric strings (frequencies, squawk codes, clearances).

Rule 5

Correct microphone technique

Hold mic 2–5 cm from mouth. Press PTT, pause 1 second, then speak. Release PTT only after finishing. Avoid breathing directly into the mic.

Rule 6

Read back mandatory items

Always read back: runway, clearance, heading, altitude, altimeter, squawk code, frequency change. Omitting a readback signals non-receipt to ATC.

Rule 7

Callsign every transmission

End every transmission with your callsign (abbreviated after first contact). This identifies who transmitted — critical on busy tower frequencies.

Rule 8

"Standby" buys you time

If you cannot respond immediately, say "Standby, [callsign]." ATC will wait. Never leave ATC unanswered — a missed call may trigger a radio-failure assumption.

Do vs Don't

✓ Do

  • Use phonetic alphabet for all letters"Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot" not "G-C-D-F"
  • Say "say again" to request a repeat"Say again altitude, C-GCDF"
  • Say "Correction" then re-transmit on errors
  • Use "Affirmative" / "Negative" for yes / no
  • Say "Wilco" when you intend to complyMeans "understood, will comply"
  • Cancel VFR flight plan on safely landing
  • Monitor 121.5 MHz on second radio when possible

✗ Don't

  • Say "repeat" — say say again (repeat = artillery)
  • Transmit personal info, profanity, or music
  • Say "Over and out" — pick one or the other
  • Use "Roger" when you mean "Wilco" — Roger = received only
  • Transmit routine calls on 121.5 MHz guard
  • Key PTT without speaking — blocks the frequency
  • Abbreviate callsign before ATC does so first
