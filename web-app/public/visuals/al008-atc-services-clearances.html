AL-008 — ATC Services and Clearances

Transport Canada · CARs 602.31, 602.32, 602.136 · AIM GEN 3.3, RAC 4.2 · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

ATC Service Types — AIM GEN 3.3
Air Traffic Control
AIM GEN 3.3 — Type 1
Prevents collisions between aircraft and between aircraft and obstructions on the manoeuvring area; expedites and maintains an orderly flow of air traffic.
Provided by: TWR, TCU/APP, ACC
Flight Information
AIM GEN 3.3 — Type 2
Provides advice and information useful for the safe and efficient conduct of flights. Includes weather, NOTAMs, airspace activity, and other operational information.
Provided by: ACC, FSS (all ATC units)
Alerting
AIM GEN 3.3 — Type 3
Notifies appropriate organizations when aircraft are in need of search and rescue assistance, and assists those organizations as required.
Provided by: All ATC units + FSS
ATC Units — Who Does What
TWR
Aerodrome Control Tower
Controls runways, taxiways, and the immediate aerodrome environment
Issues taxi instructions, take-off & landing clearances, circuit control. Sub-positions: Ground, Local, Clearance Delivery.
TCU / APP
Terminal Control Unit / Approach Control
Controls the terminal area surrounding a major airport (typically Class C airspace)
Handles departures after tower handoff; manages arrivals during approach. VFR in Class C contacts TCU.
ACC
Area Control Centre
Controls en route aircraft in controlled airspace
Canada has ACCs in Toronto, Montréal, Edmonton, Vancouver, Gander, Moncton. Handles IFR (and VFR on request) between terminal areas.
FSS
Flight Service Station
NOT an ATC unit
Weather briefings, flight plan filing, MF advisory at uncontrolled aerodromes, SAR alerting
Does not issue clearances. Provides traffic advisories at MF aerodromes.
Tower → TCU → ACC Handoff Sequence
DEPARTURE
1
Clearance Delivery / Ground
Pre-departure: taxi instructions, IFR clearance (CRAFT) at larger aerodromes
2
Tower (TWR)
Take-off clearance. Aircraft under tower control in circuit and immediate departure area
3
TCU / Approach
Tower instructs pilot to contact TCU. Handles aircraft climbing through terminal area (Class C)
4
ACC
Clear of terminal area: TCU hands off to ACC for en route phase
ARRIVAL (reverse sequence)
↓
ACC
En route → hands off to TCU as aircraft approaches terminal area
↓
TCU / Approach
Vectors aircraft for approach, hands off to tower for final
↓
Tower (TWR)
Landing clearance, taxi to parking via Ground

Readback Requirements — AIM RAC 4.2 & CARs 602.31, 602.136

IFR ClearanceRead back the entire IFR route clearance (all CRAFT elements) plus your call sign. Partial readback is not acceptable for full route clearances.
RunwayAll runway assignments and take-off / landing clearances: read back runway designation + call sign + any hold-short instruction. "Roger" alone is not sufficient. (AIM RAC 4.2)
AltimeterAltimeter settings (QNH): read back the altimeter value + call sign. Required when ATC provides a setting. (AIM RAC 4.2)
HoldsHolding instructions: read back the complete holding clearance including fix, inbound course, turn direction, and EFC time. (AIM RAC 4.2)
UnableIf a clearance would compromise safety, respond "Unable, [reason]". ATC will issue an amended clearance. Safety always overrides ATC compliance. (CARs 602.32)
ResponsibilityA correct readback confirms the instruction was received — it does not transfer responsibility. The pilot-in-command is always ultimately responsible.
