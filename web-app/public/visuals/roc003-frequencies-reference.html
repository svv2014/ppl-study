ROC-003 — Frequencies Reference

AIM COM 4.0 · Canada Flight Supplement · ISED RIC-21 · ROC-A Written Exam

Critical and Fixed Frequencies
FrequencyTypePurposeNotes
121.5 MHz DISTRESS / GUARD International aeronautical distress, ELT activation All aircraft should monitor when possible. ELTs transmit here automatically on impact. (AIM COM 4.3.1)
243.0 MHz MILITARY GUARD Military equivalent of 121.5 MHz (UHF) Not used by civil VHF radios; mil aircraft monitor. Noted in AIM.
122.8 MHz UNICOM / MF Non-towered aerodrome traffic advisories and ground-to-air communications Used at Mandatory Frequency (MF) aerodromes. Pilots self-announce position and intentions. (AIM RAC 4.5)
126.7 MHz EN ROUTE / FSS En-route advisory, FSS relay, air-to-ground away from controlled aerodromes Common enroute frequency. Also used for FSS contacts on airways. (AIM COM 4.1.4)
123.45 MHz AIR-TO-AIR GA air-to-air coordination Informal air-to-air; not for ATC contact. Widely used by GA pilots for air-to-air when in vicinity.
122.75 MHz AIR-TO-AIR (alt) Alternative air-to-air coordination frequency Used when 123.45 is congested. Check CFS for local conventions.
Frequency Bands by Service
Band / RangeServiceTypical use
118.000–136.975 MHz
VHF COM		 ATC / COM Tower, approach, departure, ground, centre (ACC). All controlled-airspace comms. 25 kHz spacing.
108.000–117.975 MHz
VHF NAV		 VOR / ILS / LOC VOR navigation, ILS localizer, marker beacons. NAV radios only — not COM. Odd tenths = VOR; even tenths = ILS loc.
ATIS (discrete) ATIS Automatic Terminal Information Service — continuous weather and NOTAM broadcast. Frequency in CFS per airport. Obtain before calling tower.
Ground Control (discrete) GROUND Taxi instructions at towered airports. Below ~118 MHz. Check CFS. Contact before taxiing.
Clearance Delivery (discrete) CLNC DEL IFR clearance pickup before pushback/taxi at busy airports. See CFS.
Canada Flight Supplement (CFS): all aerodrome frequencies are listed in the CFS entry for each airport. Always check the CFS for the correct ATIS, ground, tower, and approach frequencies before flight. Frequencies listed here are defaults — confirm locally.
EXAM HOOK
The numbers to memorise: 121.5 = International distress / ELT guard · 122.8 = Non-towered / MF · 126.7 = En route / FSS. VHF COM range is 118–137 MHz; VHF NAV is 108–118 MHz. ATIS: get it first, then call tower.
