ROC-006 — Emergency Communications

AIM COM 4.3 · CARs 602.135–602.138 · ICAO Annex 10 · ROC-A Written Exam

Emergency Transponder Codes
7700 Emergency General emergency — grave and imminent danger, immediate assistance required. Set immediately upon declaring MAYDAY.
7600 Lost Comms Radio failure — cannot receive or transmit. ATC will provide light signals. Fly expected clearance or squawk 7700 if also in distress.
7500 Hijacking Unlawful interference (hijacking in progress). Do not confirm verbally over radio unless safe to do so. ATC will initiate response.
MAYDAY vs PAN PAN

⚠ MAYDAY (×3)

Grave and imminent danger — immediate assistance required.

  • Declare for: engine failure, fire, loss of control, medical emergency with incapacitation
  • Squawk 7700
  • Transmit on 121.5 MHz if possible
  • ATC provides priority handling and may alert SAR

✎ PAN PAN (×3)

Urgency condition — safety of aircraft or person, but not immediately life-threatening.

  • Declare for: fuel state concern, passenger medical (non-incapacitating), lost (unsure of position), minor system failure
  • Squawk 7700 or as directed by ATC
  • Maintain working frequency; switch to 121.5 if no response
  • Can be upgraded to MAYDAY if situation worsens
MAYDAY — Signal Flow & Response Sequence
!
Step 1 — Declare
Transmit MAYDAY three times
"MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY — [callsign], [nature of emergency], [position], [altitude], [intentions], [souls on board], [fuel endurance]"
▶
Step 2 — Frequency
Transmit on 121.5 MHz if working frequency cannot be raised
121.5 MHz is the international distress frequency — all ATC facilities and SAR units monitor it continuously.
T
Step 3 — Squawk
Select squawk 7700 on transponder (if not already assigned a code)
Squawk 7700 alerts radar facilities immediately. ATC can track and coordinate SAR response from your radar return.
✓
Step 4 — Communicate
Maintain frequency; respond to ATC; state intentions clearly
ATC will clear other traffic and offer vectors to nearest suitable airport. Continue giving position updates as the situation develops.
✈
Step 5 — SAR Activation (if needed)
GMDSS / JRCC notified by ATC — Search and Rescue activated
Joint Rescue Coordination Centre (JRCC) coordinates SAR assets. ELT activates automatically on impact (121.5 MHz and 406 MHz digital). Provide souls on board and fuel endurance early — these determine SAR resource dispatch.

Cancel the MAYDAY: Once the emergency is resolved, cancel the distress message on the same frequency and/or on 121.5 MHz to prevent unnecessary SAR activation. State: "All stations, [callsign], cancel MAYDAY, [reason]." (AIM COM 4.3.2)
EXAM HOOK
Squawk codes: 7700 = Emergency (red alert) · 7600 = Lost comms (radio failure) · 7500 = Hijack. MAYDAY = imminent danger; PAN PAN = urgency but not yet life-threatening. Emergency frequency = 121.5 MHz. Always give: position, nature, intentions, souls on board, fuel endurance.
