ROC-006 — Emergency Communications

AIM COM 4.3 · CARs 602.135–602.138 · ICAO Annex 10 · ROC-A Written Exam

Emergency Transponder Codes
7700 Emergency Grave and imminent danger — immediate assistance required. Set immediately on declaring MAYDAY.
7600 Lost Comms Radio failure — cannot transmit or receive. ATC will provide light signals. Squawk 7700 if also in distress.
7500 Hijacking Unlawful interference in progress. Do not confirm verbally unless safe. ATC initiates response immediately.
MAYDAY vs PAN PAN

⚠ MAYDAY (×3)

Grave and imminent danger — immediate assistance required.

  • Engine failure, fire, loss of control, incapacitating medical
  • Squawk 7700
  • Transmit on 121.5 MHz if possible
  • ATC provides priority; may activate SAR

✎ PAN PAN (×3)

Urgency condition — not immediately life-threatening, but safety is at risk.

  • Fuel concern, minor medical, uncertain of position, system fault
  • Squawk 7700 or as ATC directs
  • Maintain working frequency; 121.5 if no response
  • Can be upgraded to MAYDAY if situation worsens
MAYDAY — Signal Flow (MAYDAY → GMDSS/SAR)
!
Step 1 — Declare
Transmit MAYDAY three times on working frequency
"MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY — [callsign], [nature of emergency], [position], [altitude], [intentions], [souls on board], [fuel endurance]"
▶
Step 2 — Guard Frequency
Switch to 121.5 MHz if working frequency raises no response
121.5 MHz is the international distress frequency — monitored continuously by all ATC facilities, SAR units, and most airliners.
T
Step 3 — Squawk
Select squawk 7700 on transponder
Squawk 7700 alerts all radar-equipped facilities immediately and enables ATC to vector SAR assets to your return.
✓
Step 4 — Communicate
Maintain frequency; respond to ATC; update position continuously
ATC will clear traffic and offer vectors to nearest suitable aerodrome. Continue transmitting position updates as situation evolves.
✈
Step 5 — GMDSS / SAR Activation
ATC notifies JRCC — Search and Rescue activated
Joint Rescue Coordination Centre (JRCC) dispatches SAR assets. ELT activates automatically on impact (121.5 MHz analogue + 406 MHz digital COSPAS-SARSAT). Souls on board and fuel endurance determine initial SAR resource dispatch.
