Circuit Standards

Standard Direction

Left-Hand
All turns to the left unless CFS, NOTAM, or ATC specifies right-hand

Circuit Altitude

1,000 ft AGL
Light aircraft. Some aerodromes use 1,500 ft AGL — check CFS

Standard Join

45° Mid-Downwind
From non-traffic (upwind) side, 45° angle to mid-point of downwind
Circuit Legs — In Order
#LegDescriptionKey Actions
1 Upwind Departure, same direction as runway, climbing Initial climb-out, retract flaps
2 Crosswind Perpendicular to runway, away from aerodrome Continue climb to circuit altitude
3 Downwind Parallel to runway, opposite direction to landing, at circuit altitude ATIS/altimeter, fuel, pre-landing checks
4 Base Perpendicular to runway, toward the runway Reduce speed, extend flaps, begin descent
5 Final Aligned with runway, into wind, descending to land Manage descent rate, airspeed, alignment

Key Facts for the Exam

DirectionLeft-hand turns are the Canadian standard. Right-hand only when published in CFS, NOTAM, or directed by ATC.
AltitudeStandard altitude is 1,000 ft AGL for light aircraft. Always check the CFS for aerodrome-specific info.
DownwindThe leg that runs parallel to the runway in the opposite direction to landing. Classic exam question.
JoiningRecommended join: 45° to mid-downwind from the non-traffic (upwind) side of the runway.
