AL-006 — Aerodrome Traffic Circuit

Transport Canada · CARs 602.96, 602.105, TP 12880E, AIM RAC 4.5 · PPL Written Exam

Standard Circuit — Plan View (Top-Down)
Circuit Standards

Standard Direction

Left-Hand
All turns to the left unless CFS, NOTAM, or ATC specifies right-hand

Circuit Altitude

1,000 ft AGL
Light aircraft. Some aerodromes use 1,500 ft AGL — check CFS

Standard Join

45° Mid-Downwind
From non-traffic (upwind) side, 45° to mid-point of downwind leg
Circuit Legs — In Order
#LegDescriptionKey Actions
1 Upwind Departure, same direction as runway heading, climbing Initial climb-out, retract flaps
2 Crosswind Perpendicular to runway, away from aerodrome Continue climb to 1,000 ft AGL circuit altitude
3 Downwind Parallel to runway, opposite direction to landing, at 1,000 ft AGL ATIS/altimeter, fuel, pre-landing checks; radio call abeam threshold
4 Base Perpendicular to runway, toward the runway Reduce speed, extend flaps, begin descent; radio call
5 Final Aligned with runway, into wind, descending to land Manage descent rate, airspeed, alignment; radio call

Key Facts for the Exam

Direction Left-hand turns are the Canadian standard. Right-hand only when published in CFS, NOTAM, or directed by ATC.
Altitude Standard altitude is 1,000 ft AGL for light aircraft. Always check the CFS for aerodrome-specific info.
Downwind The leg that runs parallel to the runway in the opposite direction to landing. This is the "which leg" question on the exam.
Joining Recommended join: 45° to mid-downwind from the non-traffic (upwind) side of the runway.
Radio Three call positions at controlled aerodromes: downwind abeam the threshold, turning base, and established on final (AIM RAC 4.5).
