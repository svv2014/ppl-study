SIGMET · AIRMET · GFACN — Trigger Summary

SIGMET

Hazards affecting all aircraft: severe/extreme turb, severe icing, widespread TS, volcanic ash, heavy squall lines, severe mountain waves. Valid ≤ 4 hr.

AIRMET

Hazards to light aircraft: moderate turb, moderate icing, extensive surface winds ≥ 20 kt, vis < 3 SM over wide areas, mountain obscuration. Valid ≤ 6 hr.

Urgent PIREP (UUA)

Triggered by: tornado / funnel cloud, hail ≥ ¾ in, severe turbulence, severe icing, LLWS, volcanic ash, any condition beyond forecast.

EXAM HOOK
PIREPs are the only real-time, in-cloud truth you have — forecasts lag. File one whenever conditions differ from the forecast. UUA triggers: severe turb/ice, LLWS, tornado/funnel, hail ≥ ¾ in, volcanic ash. SIGMETs amend the GFA for hazards threatening all aircraft; AIRMETs target light aircraft.
