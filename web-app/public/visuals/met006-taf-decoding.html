EXAM HOOK
FM = from, rapid & certain, replaces everything prior. BECMG = gradual & certain. TEMPO = brief fluctuations, base still applies between. PROB30/40 = chance only, never combined with FM. TAFs cover a 5-NM radius around the aerodrome for 24 hours (30 hrs for major sites) and are issued four times daily.
