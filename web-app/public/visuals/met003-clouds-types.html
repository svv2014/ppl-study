Cumulonimbus — Hazard Summary
Hazard Description Risk Level
Severe Turbulence Updrafts and downdrafts up to 10,000 fpm within the storm; gust fronts outward Extreme
All Icing Types Clear, rime, and mixed icing; supercooled water droplets throughout Severe
Lightning Every active Cb produces lightning; can strike well outside visible cloud Extreme
Hail Can be ejected up to 20 NM ahead of storm anvil at altitude Severe
Wind Shear / Microburst Severe wind shear at low altitude; microburst downdraft up to 6,000 fpm Extreme

Memory Hook — Cloud Prefixes and Families

HEIGHTAlto = middle, Cirro = high, no prefix (St, Cu, Ns) = low. Height determines the prefix.
SHAPECu = heap/puffy (cumulo = heap), Ci = hair/wispy (cirrus = curl of hair), St = sheet/layer (stratus = spread), Ns = rain (nimbus = rain).
Cb RULENever enter a Cb. Never fly under one. Clear by at least 20 NM laterally. The anvil shows downwind extent — go upwind side.
FRONT CLUESCi → Cs → As → Ns sequence = warm front approaching. Cu → Cb rapid build = cold front / afternoon convection.
