ROC-A — Standard Radio Phraseology

Canadian AIM RAC · Transport Canada · ROC-A Quick Reference — NOT FAA phraseology

Controlled Airport (Tower / Ground)
CONTROLLED — Initial Call to Ground
Taxi Request
[Airport] Ground, [call sign], at [location], request taxi, runway [##], with [ATIS info letter / weather].
Example: "Buttonville Ground, Cessna Golf-Charlie-Foxtrot-Bravo, at the main apron, request taxi, runway 15, with information Alpha."
CONTROLLED — Before Runway Departure
Takeoff Clearance Request (Contacting Tower)
[Airport] Tower, [call sign], holding short runway [##], ready for departure, [VFR / IFR].
Example: "Buttonville Tower, Cessna Golf-Charlie-Foxtrot-Bravo, holding short runway 15, ready for departure, VFR."

Note: ATC issues takeoff clearance; you do NOT depart until you hear "Cleared for takeoff runway ##."
Uncontrolled Airport — Circuit Position Reports (MF/ATF)
At an uncontrolled aerodrome with a Mandatory Frequency (MF) or Aerodrome Traffic Frequency (ATF), all pilots broadcast their intentions and position. There is no ATC — these are self-announce calls. Format: [Airport] traffic, [call sign], [position/intention], runway [##], [airport] traffic. (AIM RAC 4.5.2, TP 14371)
Taxi / Departure → Upwind → Crosswind → Downwind → Base → Final → Clear of runway
Taxiing / Departing
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], taxiing runway [##], departing [direction / to destination], [airport] traffic.
Example: "Rockcliffe traffic, Cessna Golf-Charlie-Foxtrot-Bravo, taxiing runway 09, departing to the west, Rockcliffe traffic."
Upwind (after lift-off)
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], upwind runway [##], [airport] traffic.
Crosswind
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], crosswind runway [##], [airport] traffic.
Downwind
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], left/right downwind runway [##], [airport] traffic.
Example: "Rockcliffe traffic, Cessna Golf-Charlie-Foxtrot-Bravo, left downwind runway 09, Rockcliffe traffic."
Base
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], left/right base runway [##], [airport] traffic.
Final
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], final runway [##], [airport] traffic.
Example: "Rockcliffe traffic, Cessna Golf-Charlie-Foxtrot-Bravo, final runway 09, Rockcliffe traffic."
Clear of Active Runway (after landing)
[Airport] traffic, [call sign], clear of runway [##], taxiing to parking, [airport] traffic.
Arriving aircraft (first call entering the MF/ATF zone):
"[Airport] traffic, [call sign], [aircraft type], [position relative to airport, altitude], inbound for landing, runway [##], [airport] traffic."

Always end self-announce calls by repeating the airport name — this helps pilots on other frequencies recognise which airport the call belongs to. (Source: AIM RAC 4.5.2)
