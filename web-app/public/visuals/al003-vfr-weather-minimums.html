AL-003 — VFR Weather Minimums

Transport Canada · CARs 602.114–602.117 · PPL Written Exam

Quick Reference

Airspace Condition Visibility Below Cloud Above Cloud Horizontal
CDE Day 3 SM 500 ft 1,000 ft 2,000 ft
CDE Night 🌙 5 SM 500 ft 1,000 ft 2,000 ft
G ≤1,000 AGL Day 2 SM Clear of Cloud
G >1,000 AGL Day 1 SM ⚠️ 500 ft 1,000 ft 2,000 ft
G Night 🌙 3 SM 500 ft 1,000 ft 2,000 ft

5-Tier Memory Framework

1
Controlled Day (C/D/E): 3 SM · 500 below · 1,000 above · 2,000 horizontal — the baseline
2
Controlled Night (C/D/E): 5 SM · same cloud clearance — only visibility goes up
3
Class G below 1,000 AGL — Day: 2 SM · clear of cloud — simple and loose
4
Class G above 1,000 AGL — Day: 1 SM · 500-1,000-2,000 — lowest visibility in the regs ⚠️
5
Class G Night: 3 SM · 500-1,000-2,000 — same as controlled day visibility

⚠️ Common Exam Trap

Class G above 1,000 ft AGL during the day only requires 1 SM visibility — lower than controlled airspace. Students expect higher altitude = stricter rules, but Class G above 1,000 ft is the exception. The cloud clearance distances are the same as controlled airspace, but visibility is just 1 SM.
