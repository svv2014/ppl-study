ROC-004 — Position Reporting

AIM COM 4.0 · AIM RAC 3.7 · ICAO Annex 10 · ROC-A Written Exam

Standard Position Report — 7 Elements
1
Aircraft identification
Your callsign as filed (or abbreviated if ATC has done so).
"Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot"
2
Position
Named waypoint, fix, or distance/bearing from a known point.
"over GOSPO intersection" / "five miles north of Smiths Falls"
3
Time (UTC)
Time at position — minutes only if within the hour.
"at two three" (0023Z) or "at one four three zero" (1430Z)
4
Altitude or Flight Level
Cruising altitude ASL or flight level. State climbing/descending if applicable.
"five thousand five hundred" / "flight level one eight zero"
5
Type of flight plan
VFR or IFR (when relevant to context or required by ATC).
"VFR"
6
Next reporting point and ETA
The next waypoint and estimated arrival time.
"estimating Pembroke at four five"
7
Remarks (if any)
Operationally significant information: weather deviations, turbulence, fuel state.
"light turbulence at this level"
Example Position Report
"Ottawa Radio, Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot, over Almonte at three five, five thousand five hundred VFR, estimating Pembroke at five two, nil remarks."
When Position Reports Are Required
SituationRequirementFrequency
IFR — compulsory reporting points Mandatory ATC/FSS as assigned
VFR with flight plan — points noted in plan Mandatory FSS (126.7 or local)
Arriving/departing MF aerodrome MF Required — announce intentions 122.8 MHz
Within ATZ at MF aerodrome MF Required — circuit positions 122.8 MHz
VFR in controlled airspace ATC Directed — when requested Assigned ATC frequency
MF vs UNICOM: At a Mandatory Frequency (MF) aerodrome, pilots broadcast position and intentions on 122.8 MHz — not to receive a clearance, but to share traffic information. No ATC clearance exists at an MF field. Monitor the MF within the MF area and broadcast 5 minutes before entering.

Memory Hook

7ID → Position → Time → Altitude → Flight type → Next fix + ETA → Remarks
