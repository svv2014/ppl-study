ROC-004 — Position Reporting

AIM COM 4.0 · AIM RAC 3.7 · ICAO Annex 10 · ROC-A Written Exam

Standard Position Report — 7 Elements
1
Aircraft identification
Your callsign as filed (or abbreviated if ATC has abbreviated).
"Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot"
2
Position
Named waypoint, VOR, fix, or distance & bearing from known point.
"over GOSPO intersection" / "five miles north of Smiths Falls"
3
Time
UTC time at position (minutes only if within the hour).
"at two three" (0023Z) or "at one four three zero" (1430Z)
4
Altitude or Flight Level
Cruising altitude (ASL) or flight level. State if climbing/descending.
"five thousand five hundred" / "flight level one eight zero"
5
Type of flight plan
VFR or IFR (if relevant to context / required by ATC).
"VFR"
6
Next reporting point and ETA
The next waypoint and estimated time of arrival there.
"estimating Pembroke at four five"
7
Remarks (if required)
Anything operationally significant: weather deviations, souls on board if declaring urgency, fuel state.
"light turbulence at this altitude"
Example Position Report
"Ottawa Radio, Golf Charlie Delta Foxtrot, over Almonte at three five, five thousand five hundred VFR, estimating Pembroke at five two, nil remarks."
When Are Position Reports Required?
SituationRequirementFrequency
IFR flight — compulsory reporting points Mandatory ATC / FSS (as assigned)
VFR flight with a flight plan — reporting points noted in plan Mandatory at each point FSS (126.7 or local)
Arriving at or departing from an MF aerodrome MF Required — announce intentions 122.8 MHz
Operating within ATZ (Aerodrome Traffic Zone) at MF field MF Required — circuit positions 122.8 MHz
VFR flight in controlled airspace with ATC ATC Directed — when requested Assigned ATC frequency
MF vs UNICOM: A Mandatory Frequency (MF) aerodrome requires pilots to broadcast position and intentions on 122.8 MHz — not to receive a clearance, but to share traffic information. No ATC clearance exists at an MF field; it is a self-announce system. Always monitor the MF frequency within the MF area and broadcast 5 minutes before entering.
EXAM HOOK
7-element report order: ID → Position → Time → Altitude → Flight type → Next fix + ETA → Remarks. At MF aerodromes use 122.8 MHz and self-announce — no clearance exists. VFR flight plans require position reports to FSS at each reported point.
