NAV-005 — Wind Correction Angle and Ground Speed

Transport Canada · TP 12880E Ch.9 · PPL Written Exam

The Wind Triangle

The wind triangle shows three vectors. The E6B solves for WCA and GS automatically.

True Airspeed (TAS) — aircraft's speed through the air mass, in your heading direction
Wind vector — speed and direction the air mass moves (from winds aloft forecast)
Desired track — the actual path over the ground you want to fly
WCA — angle between your heading and desired track; crab into the wind
Wind Effect on Ground Speed
Wind Angle to TrackGS EffectExample (TAS 100 kt, Wind 20 kt)
Direct Headwind (180° to track)GS = TAS − wind speedGS = 100 − 20 = 80 kt
Direct Tailwind (000° to track)GS = TAS + wind speedGS = 100 + 20 = 120 kt
Pure Crosswind (090° or 270°)WCA correction only; GS ≈ TASGS ≈ 100 kt; WCA ≈ 12°
Quartering headwindWCA correction + GS decreasesSolve with E6B or formula

WCA Approximation Formula

WCA ≈ (XW ÷ TAS) × 60

Where XW is the crosswind component (wind speed × sin of angle between wind and track). Good for quick mental checks — exact solution requires E6B or flight computer.

Wind Direction Convention

  • Winds aloft forecast — direction wind is FROM, in True degrees
  • Surface wind METAR/ATIS — direction FROM, in Magnetic degrees
  • "270/20" = wind FROM 270° (west) at 20 kt
  • Convert winds aloft to Magnetic before solving triangle

Worked Example — Ottawa to Kingston

True Track:247°
TAS:100 kt
Winds Aloft (True):290°/25 kt — wind from northwest
Wind angle to track:290° − 247° = 43° off the left side
WCA (E6B result):+11° (turn right / crab right into wind)
True Heading:247° + 11° = 258°
Ground Speed:86 kt (headwind component reduces GS)

Exam Memory Hooks

CRABWind from left → crab left (subtract WCA from track). Wind from right → crab right (add WCA). Always crab into the wind.
GSUse Ground Speed for time and fuel calculations. GS = how fast you actually move over the ground. TAS is only your speed through the air mass.
E6BThe E6B wind side takes: Wind direction & speed + True Airspeed + True Track → outputs WCA and GS. Set wind, then rotate disc to true course.
