Why altered pronunciations? ICAO chose these to reduce confusion over radio when English is spoken by non-native speakers or in noisy RF environments. "TREE" (3) can't be mistaken for "free"; "FIFE" (5) is distinct from "fire"; "NIN-er" (9) avoids confusion with the German "nein" (no).

Hundreds and thousands: Each digit is spoken individually. Altitude 3,500 ft = "TREE FIFE ZE-RO ZE-RO". Frequency 126.7 MHz = "WUN TOO SIX DECIMAL SEV-en". (Source: AIM RAC 1.4, ICAO Doc 9432)
