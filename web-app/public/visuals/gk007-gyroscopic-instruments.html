GK-007 — Gyroscopic Instruments

Transport Canada · TP 12880E · PPL Written Exam

The Three Gyroscopic Instruments
Attitude Indicator
AI — ARTIFICIAL HORIZON
Shows aircraft pitch and bank attitude relative to the horizon. Rigidity in space keeps gyro fixed. Allows flight reference in IMC. Vacuum powered (typically) or electric on some aircraft.
Heading Indicator
HI — DIRECTIONAL GYRO (DI)
Shows magnetic heading via gyro stability. Does NOT sense magnetic north directly — must be set to match magnetic compass. Precesses ~3°/hr — must reset every 15 minutes. Free of compass turning/acceleration errors.
Turn Coordinator
TC — TURN & SLIP INDICATOR
Shows rate and quality of turn. Miniature aircraft indicates turn rate. Inclinometer ball: centred = coordinated; skid = ball to outside; slip = ball to inside. Standard rate = 3°/sec (360° in 2 min).

Vacuum System Note

Engine-driven vacuum pumpCreates suction to spin gyros in AI and HI via filtered air flow

Exam Key Points: The magnetic compass is the only instrument that directly senses magnetic north — the HI must be aligned to it periodically. In a vacuum failure, both AI and HI become unreliable; only the electric Turn Coordinator and magnetic compass remain. The ball in the inclinometer always falls to the low side of a slip/skid — "step on the ball" to correct.
