Memory Hook — Classes at a Glance

AIFR only — 18,000 ft and up. VFR not permitted. IFR rating required.
BClearance required for everyone — 12,500–17,999 ft ASL. VFR is permitted with clearance. Not IFR-only (unlike US). Mode C required.
CClearance + radio — TCAs around major airports. ATC separates IFR from VFR, not VFR from VFR.
DRadio contact only — control zones at towered airports. Must establish 2-way before entry; no clearance needed for VFR.
ENo requirements for VFR — but it's still controlled airspace. IFR aircraft are on ATC clearance; you're on your own (see and avoid).
FSpecial use — F(R) restricted (need authorization); F(A) advisory (hazard warning). Always check NOTAM and CFS.
GUncontrolled — no ATC. Ceiling is 700 ft AGL in southern transition areas, 1,200 ft AGL elsewhere. No requirements; lower weather minima.
