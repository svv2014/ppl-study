Critical Limits to Memorize: Oil pressure must be in the green within 30 seconds of starting (cold) or engine must be shut down. Low oil pressure + high oil temperature = likely oil leak or pump failure — land immediately. EGT is used to lean the mixture: richest mixture at peak EGT minus ~50°F (rich of peak) or specific lean-of-peak per POH. Always refer to the specific aircraft POH for actual numerical limits.
