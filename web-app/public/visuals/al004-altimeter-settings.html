Flight Levels
QNE — 29.92 inHg

All aircraft use standard pressure. Altimeter reads pressure altitude. Expressed as FL180, FL200, etc. IFR only above this level.

< 18,000 ft ASL
VFR operations
Altitude ASL
QNH — local sea-level pressure

Use the current altimeter setting from the nearest station. Altimeter reads altitude above sea level. Update when crossing regions.

Memory Hooks

High → LowFlying into lower pressure without resetting: your true altitude is lower than indicated — the ground is closer than you think. "High to low, look out below."
Setting UpTurning the Kollsman knob higher increases indicated altitude. Turning it lower decreases indicated altitude.
QNH vs QNEQNH changes constantly (local weather); QNE is always 29.92 inHg (standard, fixed).
