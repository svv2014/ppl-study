AL-009 — Flight Plans & Itineraries

Transport Canada · CARs 602.73–602.78, TP 12880E, AIM RAC 3.6 · PPL Written Exam

Flight Plan (ATC) vs. Flight Itinerary (SAR) — CARs 602.73–602.75
Flight Plan

Filed with ATC / FSS — CARs 602.73

  • Submitted to an ATC unit or Flight Service Station before departure
  • Activates SAR alerting automatically if overdue on arrival
  • Required for all IFR flights
  • Required for VFR flights entering Class B airspace
  • Recommended for any overwater or remote VFR flight
  • Must be closed with ATC/FSS on arrival — failure to close triggers an unnecessary SAR response (CARs 602.77)
Flight Itinerary

Left with a Responsible Person — CARs 602.75

  • Provided to a responsible person on the ground (friend, FBO, employer) before departure
  • Responsible person notifies the nearest JRCC or ATC unit if pilot is overdue
  • Required for VFR flights over remote or uninhabited terrain where a flight plan is not filed (CARs 602.75)
  • No formal closing procedure — notify the responsible person upon safe arrival
  • SAR not automatically alerted — depends entirely on responsible person acting
When a Flight Plan is Required — CARs 602.73
Flight TypeFlight Plan Required?Notes
IFR — any route Yes — always No exceptions. Must be filed before departure.
VFR — Class B airspace Yes Class B requires a clearance, which requires a flight plan.
VFR — Class C/D controlled Not required Clearance needed to enter, but no flight plan mandatory.
VFR — remote / uninhabited terrain Flight plan or itinerary CARs 602.73(2): must have one or the other over hostile terrain.
VFR — local / non-remote Not required Recommended practice for any cross-country flight.
Filing Deadlines
IFR: File at least 30 minutes before estimated off-block time (EOBT) — AIM Canada RAC 3.6.
VFR: File before departure. Class B requires the plan to be filed and a clearance obtained before entering the airspace.
Closing (CARs 602.77): The pilot-in-command must close the flight plan by radio or phone to ATC/FSS immediately after landing. If the plan is not closed within 1 hour of ETA, ATC/FSS declares the aircraft overdue and initiates SAR procedures.

SAR Notification Timelines

When Search and Rescue is Alerted

Flight Plan Aircraft is considered overdue when it has not arrived and the flight plan has not been closed within 1 hour of the filed ETA. ATC/FSS initiates the overdue aircraft procedure (ALERFA → DETRESFA).
Itinerary The responsible person must notify the nearest JRCC (Joint Rescue Coordination Centre) or ATC unit if the aircraft is overdue — within 24 hours of ETA or at the time specified in the itinerary. SAR is not automatically notified.
Closing Pilot-in-command must close the flight plan by radio, phone, or in person with ATC/FSS immediately on arrival. Failure to close is an offence under CARs 602.77 and triggers an unnecessary SAR response.

Key Facts for the Exam

IFR = PlanIFR flights always require a flight plan — no exceptions. VFR flights require a plan or itinerary only when over remote or uninhabited terrain.
Always CloseFailure to close a flight plan is a CARs offence and wastes SAR resources. Close by phone (1-800-WXBRIEF) or radio to FSS/ATC.
Itinerary ≠ PlanAn itinerary left with a responsible person does not automatically alert SAR — that person must take action. A flight plan does alert SAR automatically when overdue.
Field 19Field 19 covers emergency and survival information: fuel endurance, persons on board, survival equipment, ELT type — know what goes here.
