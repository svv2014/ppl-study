MET-011 — Turbulence

Transport Canada · TP 12880E · AIM MET 2.6

Intensity Scale
LGT — Light
Slight, erratic attitude changes. Occupants feel strain against belts. Walking possible.
MOD — Moderate
Positive control maintained. Definite strain against belts. Unsecured items shift. Walking difficult.
SEV — Severe
Large abrupt changes in attitude/altitude. Momentary loss of control. Loose objects tossed. UUA report.
EXT — Extreme
Aircraft violently tossed, practically impossible to control. Structural damage possible. UUA report.
Four Causes

Mechanical

Wind over rough terrain or structures
  • Strongest ≥ 20 kt winds over hills/ridges
  • Leeward-side rotor & standing waves
  • Avoid by: upwind flight, higher altitude

Convective (Thermal)

Unstable air + surface heating
  • Summer afternoons over dark surfaces
  • CU fair-weather bumps to CB severity
  • Avoid by: early-morning flight, climb above CU tops

Frontal

Air-mass interface
  • Worst at fast cold fronts with strong uplift
  • Can mask embedded TS
  • Avoid by: delay crossing, climb above the lift zone

Wind-Shear / CAT

Sudden wind velocity/direction change
  • Near jet streams, inversions, mountain waves
  • Clear Air Turbulence invisible; watch for PIREPs
  • Avoid by: altitude change ±2000 ft
Mountain Waves — Signature Pattern
SignIndicates
Lenticular clouds (smooth, lens-shaped)Standing wave crest in stable air.
Rotor clouds (ragged, cumulus-like, under the crest)Very strong, low-level turbulence — severe to extreme.
Cap cloud over ridge topStrong upslope flow; downslope turbulence on lee side.
Wind ≥ 25 kt perpendicular to ridgeMountain wave likely; add ≥ 50% of ridge height to crossing altitude.
Airspeed in Turbulence
SpeedReason
Slow to Va (manoeuvring speed) or belowAt Va, the wing stalls before the airframe is overstressed, even at full deflection.
Keep attitude levelChasing altitude spikes overstresses the aircraft more than riding them out.
Belts tight, loose items securedInjury prevention in MOD or worse.
EXAM HOOK
Four turbulence causes: Mechanical · Convective · Frontal · Shear/CAT. Slow to Va (manoeuvring speed) and below when MOD or worse — wing stalls before airframe breaks. Lenticular + rotor clouds = mountain wave with severe low-level turbulence. Any SEV or EXT turbulence = file a UUA PIREP.
