EXAM HOOK
All weather is in the troposphere. Temperature drops with altitude at ≈ 2 °C per 1,000 ft (ISA), pressure drops ≈ 1 inHg per 1,000 ft near the surface. Above the tropopause (≈ 36,000 ft over Canada), the stratosphere is isothermal then warms — smooth air, no clouds, no weather.
