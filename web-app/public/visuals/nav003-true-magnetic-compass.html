Variation vs Deviation

  • Variation — Earth's magnetic field offset from geographic north; shown as isogonic lines on VNC; applies to all aircraft in the area
  • Deviation — Aircraft-specific error caused by onboard metal, electronics, magnets; recorded on the compass deviation card in the cockpit
  • Both use same rule: East → subtract, West → add

Three Norths

  • True North — Geographic North Pole; basis of VNC charts and most navigation calculations
  • Magnetic North — Where compass needles point; currently near Ellesmere Island; moves ~25 km/year
  • Compass North — What your aircraft compass reads; affected by local magnetic disturbances (deviation)

Exam Memory Hooks

RULEEast is least, West is best — Easterly variation: subtract (less) to convert True→Magnetic. Westerly: add (more).
CHAINTo go from Compass → True: add back East, subtract West (reverse the chain). Useful when reading a compass and wanting True track.
CHARTIsogonic lines on the VNC are labelled "15°E" or "20°W" — the label tells you the variation directly. Lines of zero variation are called agonic lines.
