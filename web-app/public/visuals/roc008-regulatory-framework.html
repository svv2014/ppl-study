ROC-008 — Radio Regulatory Framework

Radiocommunication Act · ISED RIC-21 · ICAO Annex 10 · ROC-A Written Exam

Regulatory Pillars

Radiocommunication Act (Canada) ISED

Federal statute administered by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED). Governs all radio transmitters in Canada, including aircraft.

  • Prohibits operating a radio transmitter without a valid licence or authorization
  • Aeronautical radio operators must hold a Restricted Operator Certificate — Aeronautical (ROC-A)
  • Aircraft must carry an Aeronautical Station Licence (issued by ISED)
  • The ROC-A is an ISED certificate — separate from Transport Canada pilot licensing

Restricted Operator Certificate — Aeronautical (ROC-A) ISED

The individual certificate required by any person who operates an aeronautical mobile radio station (the aircraft radio).

  • Examined and issued by ISED-accredited examiners
  • Tests: radio operation, phonetic alphabet, distress procedures, frequencies, regulations
  • Valid for life once issued — no renewal required
  • Not the same as a pilot licence — a non-pilot ground operator also needs one
  • A student pilot must operate under supervision of a ROC-A holder until they obtain one

Aeronautical Station Licence ISED

The station (aircraft) licence for radio equipment installed in the aircraft.

  • Required for Canadian-registered aircraft; must be carried on board
  • The pilot certificate + station licence satisfy ROC-A operational requirements

Canadian Aviation Regulations (CARs) TC

Transport Canada regulations governing aviation operations, including when radio use is required per airspace class.

  • CARs Part VI sets radio requirements per airspace class (see ROC-005)
  • Phraseology standards in AIM supplement the CARs
  • TC enforces CARs; ISED enforces Radiocommunication Act — they are separate agencies

ICAO Annex 10 — Aeronautical Telecommunications ICAO

International standards adopted by Canada. Sets phonetic alphabet, distress phraseology, and frequency allocations globally.

  • Annex 10 Vol. II covers communication procedures
  • Canada's domestic rules must meet or exceed ICAO standards

ROC-A vs Pilot Licence — two separate credentials: ISED issues the ROC-A (operator cert) and the Aeronautical Station Licence (aircraft licence). Transport Canada issues the pilot licence. You need both to legally operate an aircraft radio in Canada. (Radiocommunication Act s. 13)
