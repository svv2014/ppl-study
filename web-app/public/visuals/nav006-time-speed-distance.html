Transport Canada · TP 12880E Ch.9 · PPL Written Exam

TSD Formula Triangle
Find Distance:
D = S × T
Speed (kt) × Time (hours) = NM
Find Speed / Ground Speed:
S = D ÷ T
Distance (NM) ÷ Time (hours) = knots
Find Time (in minutes):
T = D ÷ S × 60
Multiply by 60 to convert hours → minutes

The 60:1 Rule — Mental Shortcut

GS (kt) ÷ 60 = NM per minute

At 60 kt, you cover exactly 1 NM per minute. At any other speed, divide by 60. This mental shortcut lets you verify checkpoint times quickly without a calculator.

Example: Flying at 90 kt → 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 NM/min → a 15 NM leg takes 10 minutes.

Exam Memory Hooks

UNITSThe TSD formula uses hours. If you want minutes, multiply the result by 60, or divide minutes by 60 before plugging in. Forgetting this is the most common TSD error.
SPEEDAlways use Ground Speed, not TAS, for time and distance calculations. GS accounts for wind effect over the ground.
ETAETA = departure time + time en route. If you depart 1320Z and the leg takes 47 min, ETA = 1320 + 47 = 1407Z. Watch for hour rollovers.
