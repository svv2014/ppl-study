NAV-006 — Time-Speed-Distance Calculations

Transport Canada · TP 12880E Ch.9 · PPL Written Exam

The TSD Triangle
Three Formulas — One Triangle

Distance

D = S × T
Speed × Time (hrs)

Example: GS = 120 kt, T = 45 min
Convert: 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 hr
D = 120 × 0.75 = 90 NM

Speed

S = D ÷ T
Distance ÷ Time (hrs)

Example: D = 80 NM, T = 40 min
Convert: 40 ÷ 60 = 0.667 hr
S = 80 ÷ 0.667 = 120 kt

Time

T = D ÷ S
Distance ÷ Speed → hours

Example: D = 75 NM, S = 100 kt
T = 75 ÷ 100 = 0.75 hr
0.75 × 60 = 45 min

Memory Hooks

Key Concepts to Remember

60-TO-1 The 60-to-1 rule simplifies mental math: at 60 kt GS you cover 1 NM per minute. At 120 kt you cover 2 NM/min. Use this for quick checkpoint estimates in the cockpit.
CONVERT Always convert minutes to hours before multiplying or dividing. Divide minutes by 60. Exam questions often give time in minutes — forgetting to convert is the #1 arithmetic mistake.
EXAM TIP Cover the unknown variable in the triangle: what remains shows the operation. Top variable (D) = multiply the two bottom variables. Bottom variable = divide D by the other bottom variable.
