Force Descriptions
ForceSourceDirection
LiftWing airfoil (Bernoulli + Newton)Perpendicular to relative wind, upward
WeightGravity acting on aircraft massStraight down toward Earth centre
ThrustEngine + propellerForward, along flight path
DragAerodynamic resistance (parasite + induced)Opposite to direction of flight
Memory Hook — "LDTW" (Level Down Thrust Weight)
Think of two pairs: Lift/Weight (vertical axis) and Thrust/Drag (horizontal axis).
In level, unaccelerated flight: Lift = Weight AND Thrust = Drag.
Climbing requires excess lift OR reduced weight — but in sustained climb, thrust must overcome both drag and the weight component along the flight path.
Induced drag increases with angle of attack (slow flight). Parasite drag increases with airspeed squared.
