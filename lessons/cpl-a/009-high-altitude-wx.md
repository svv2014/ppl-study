# Lesson CAM-002: High-Altitude Weather and Jet Stream Effects

**Subject:** Advanced Meteorology  
**Status:** Draft — content pending

This lesson covers weather phenomena relevant to higher-altitude commercial VFR and IFR-adjacent operations: jet stream position and effects, clear-air turbulence (CAT), mountain wave activity, upper wind forecasts (UA forms), and the practical use of NAV CANADA's graphical upper wind charts. Distinct from PPL-A meteorology in its focus on flight levels and higher-altitude planning rather than low-level VFR.
