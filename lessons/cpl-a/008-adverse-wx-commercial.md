# Lesson CAM-001: Adverse Weather Decision-Making for Commercial Operations

**Subject:** Advanced Meteorology  
**Status:** Draft — content pending

**PPL-A Foundation:** PPL-A lesson MET-014 (weather-decision-making) covers the go/no-go framework for personal VFR flight. This CPL-A lesson extends that foundation to commercial operations: operator minimums, dispatch authority, crew communication during deteriorating conditions, and the legal and professional consequences of continuing into adverse weather.

New content beyond PPL-A MET-014: commercial operator obligations under CARs Part VII, the role of meteorological dispatch, in-flight weather reassessment obligations, and case studies from TC safety publications.

Content to author: body narration, 5 quiz questions, source citations.
