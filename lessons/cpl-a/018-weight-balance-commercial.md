# Lesson CAP-002: Weight and Balance — Multi-Seat and Multi-Leg Operations

**Subject:** Performance and Limitations  
**Status:** Draft — content pending

**PPL-A Foundation:** PPL-A lesson GK-009 (weight-and-balance) covers CG concepts, moment calculations, and the loading envelope. This CPL-A lesson extends to: multi-passenger loading optimization, fuel burn and CG shift during a multi-leg flight (forward/aft CG migration), freight loading and floor loading limits, the use of loading manifests required for commercial flights, and the PIC's legal responsibility to confirm compliance before each departure regardless of dispatch-provided loading data.
