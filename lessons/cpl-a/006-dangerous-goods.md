# Lesson CAL-006: Dangerous Goods in Air Transport

**Status:** Draft skeleton — content pending.

This lesson will cover classification, packaging, labelling, and documentation requirements for dangerous goods carried by air under CARs Part XII and the ICAO Technical Instructions, including UN hazard classes, operator/shipper responsibilities, and the PIC's right to refuse dangerous goods.
