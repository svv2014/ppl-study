About This Curriculum

60 lessons × 20 minutes each = approximately 8–9 weeks at one lesson per day. Lessons cover all four Transport Canada PPL exam topic areas. Audio narration is available for completed lessons.

Minimum passing grade: 60%. Target: 80%+. Format: ~100 multiple-choice questions, 3.5 hours.
